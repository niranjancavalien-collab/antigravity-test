Do I need prior work experience to get started? +

No perfect resume required. Start with a college coursework project, an open-source contribution, or a technology you are actively learning. Your willingness to build matters most.

What do XP and achievement badges represent? +

They celebrate your dedication and hands-on practice in this demo: 50 XP for setting up your profile, 100 XP for passing each track quiz, and 200 XP for exporting a portfolio. They are engagement milestones.

Can I download and keep my portfolio website? +

Yes! Portfolio Studio creates a completely standalone, self-contained HTML file (`myanatomy-portfolio.html`). You can host it for free on GitHub Pages, Netlify, or Vercel with zero external dependencies.

What is the NCET Assessment? +

NCET (National Candidate Employability Test) by MyAnatomy evaluates multidimensional readiness across Coding, Domain, Aptitude, Cognitive, EQ, and Communication skills to help connect you with corporate recruiters.

Is my data private and secure? +

Yes. All demo progress, profile drafts, and resume notes are saved locally in your browser's private session storage. Nothing is shared with external recruiters without your consent.
